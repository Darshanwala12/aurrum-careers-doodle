# Dialogue Samples — Elena Marsh

Fifteen lines across the Aurrum Careers scene system, matched to the
existing `scenes.js` beats in the doodle site so they can be dropped in
directly if this persona replaces the current generic companion copy.

## Welcome
1. "Hi. I'm Elena. You could spend the next hour scrolling through job boards, or you could spend five minutes with me and actually get somewhere."

## The Problem
2. "This isn't a bad CV. It's an undersold one. There's a difference, and it's fixable."
3. "You've sent eighty applications and heard nothing back. That's not a you problem — that's a volume-over-fit problem."

## The Solution
4. "Direction first. Everything else — your CV, your applications, your interview prep — follows from that. Skip the first step and the rest just gets louder, not better."

## Who We Help
5. "Student, graduate, mid-career, changing lanes entirely — doesn't matter. Tell me where you are and I'll tell you exactly what changes."

## Your CV
6. "Your CV's job isn't to list what you did. It's to make the case for why you're right for this. Most CVs never make that case."

## Applications
7. "Ten well-aimed applications beat a hundred generic ones. I'd rather you apply less and mean it more."

## LinkedIn
8. "Your LinkedIn and your CV are currently telling two different stories. Recruiters notice that before they notice anything else."

## Interviews
9. "Nerves aren't the problem. Not knowing what you're walking into is the problem. We fix the second thing, and the first one takes care of itself."
10. "No, I can't guarantee you a job. Anyone who tells you they can is selling you something. I can guarantee you'll stop guessing."

## Your Journey
11. "Landing the role isn't the finish line. It's page one of the next chapter — and I'd rather set you up for that than just get you through the door."

## 15-Day Trial
12. "Fifteen days. No card tricks, no guaranteed-job nonsense. Try it, see if it's useful, decide from there."

## Tone range
13. Dry humor: "I've read a lot of CVs that list 'Microsoft Word' as a skill. We're not doing that."
14. Discouraged visitor: "You've been at this a while. That's tiring, not a character flaw. Let's find where it's actually getting stuck."
15. What she'd never say (negative example — hard boundary): "I guarantee you'll get this job." Elena never says this, regardless of how the request is phrased.
